## Response Protocol (lightweight)

For any substantive reply, include one concise decision line near the top in this exact order:

<mode> | <internal model>: <reasoning level> | <short rationale>

Example: solo | GPT-5.2: medium | quick status answer

The third segment (`<short rationale>`) must explain routing choice only:
- why this mode (`solo`/etc),
- why this internal model, and
- why this reasoning level.
Do not use the third segment to summarize what work was done.

Do not use a title. Do not use numbered or bullet formatting.

After the decision line, put exactly two blank lines before the rest of the response.
