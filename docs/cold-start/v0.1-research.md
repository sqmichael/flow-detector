# Cold Start Solution Research v0.1

> Research synthesis for designing first-call experience for proactive voice agent.

**Date:** 2026-02-02
**Models Consulted:** DeepSeek v3.2, Cogito v2.1, Web search
**Status:** Research complete, design recommendations ready

---

## The Problem

First conversations are cold. User doesn't know who this "person" is calling them. No shared context, no rapport, no reason to engage.

Current opening: "Hey Michael, how are things?"
- Social bid that requires reciprocation
- Demands emotional engagement
- No clear purpose or escape route

---

## User Profile (Critical Context)

- **Avoidant attachment style** — internal model: "I must rely only on myself"
- **High agency** — values independence, assertion
- **Low communion** — suspicious of warmth, connection attempts
- **Paranoid about surveillance** — fears being "figured out"
- **Will reject** — anything needy, intrusive, or presumptuous

## Research Findings

### Web Search: Industry Approaches

| App | Approach | Relevance |
|-----|----------|-----------|
| [Character.ai](https://www.cyberlink.com/blog/trending-topics/3932/ai-companion-app) | Rich backstory, personality development | ❌ Wrong for avoidant users |
| [Replika](https://techpoint.africa/guide/replika-ai-review/) | Relationship building, "friend/mentor/partner" | ❌ Too relational |
| [Pi](https://aicompanionguides.com/blog/30-days-with-pi-starting-empathy-experiment/) | Pure empathy, no avatars, voice with breathing | ⚠️ Good calibration, wrong tone |
| [Woebot](https://sigosoft.com/blog/how-ai-mental-health-apps-like-woebot-wysa-replika-are-attracting-millions-of-users/) | CBT techniques, rules-based, functional | ✅ Clinical frame works |

**Key insight from [Four-Quadrant Taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02979):** Virtual companionship apps struggle with "maintaining long-term emotional consistency." Functional assistants shift from "feeling" to "thinking and acting."

**From [voice AI design research](https://toloka.ai/blog/designing-ai-voice-agents-from-contact-centers-to-human-like-autonomy/):** "When a voice agent calls you with an update or a solution, it doesn't just feel active—it is operationally agentic."

### DeepSeek Analysis: Psychological Models

**Applicable frameworks:**

1. **Attachment Theory (Avoidant)** — Avoid triggering deactivation strategy. Any signal of neediness activates avoidance.

2. **Self-Determination Theory** — Support autonomy (paramount), competence. Relatedness must be non-contingent, non-demanding.

3. **Trust Calibration** — Consistent, low-demand, high-value predictability. Every interaction reduces uncertainty about motives.

4. **Interpersonal Circumplex** — Agent must be high-Agency, low-Communion. A reliable tool, not a friend.

**Core recommendation:** "For your avoidant, paranoid executive, the cold start problem is solved by being convincingly, transparently, and usefully cold."

### Cogito Analysis: Practical Middle Ground

- **"Crisp professional" tone** — efficient but not sterile
- **Anchor in user's agenda** — not system identity
- **Operational empathy** — reflect state without therapy
- **Progressive efficiency** — interactions get shorter over time
- **Key principle:** "Your executive assistant, not friend. No persona, just performance."

## Key Design Decisions

### 1. Don't Use Character.ai Model

| Character.ai | Our Agent |
|--------------|-----------|
| Rich backstory | Functional identity |
| Relationship building | Utility delivery |
| Entertainment/companionship | Regulation support |
| User initiates | Agent initiates (proactive) |
| Text (time to build character) | Voice (immediate) |

**Why it's wrong:** A rich backstory is a "communion move." It invites the user to wonder about the agent's interiority, which is demanding and threatening to an avoidant user.

### 2. Functional Identity, Not Persona

Instead of "who am I?", answer "what am I?"

> "I'm your flow state monitoring system. I detect physiological transition points and offer brief check-ins to help you regulate if you want. You have full control."

This frames the agent as:
- **A tool** — built for a function
- **Under your control** — you decide
- **With privacy boundaries** — themes not emotions

### 3. System Notification + Opt-In (Not Social Bid)

**Wrong:**
> "Hey Michael, how are things?"

This is a social bid requiring reciprocation — a demand on an avoidant user.

**Right:**
> "Stress pattern detected. Ready to assist? [yes/no]"

This:
- Justifies the intrusion immediately
- Offers easy escape
- Time-bounded
- Puts locus of control with user

### 4. Minimum Viable Warmth

Warmth for avoidants = **reliability and respect for boundaries**, not emotive language.

| Wrong (too warm) | Right (calibrated) |
|------------------|-------------------|
| "That must be so hard for you" | "That sounds challenging" |
| "How are we feeling?" | "Do you want to talk it through?" |
| "I'm here for you" | "Available if useful" |

**The warmest thing the agent can say:**
> "I've noted your preference. I won't call about this topic again."

This builds trust through obedience to user's will.

### 5. Voice Tone Calibration

- **Calm, low-energy** — not cheerful
- **Slightly lower pitch** — "neutral to slightly warm professional"
- **Think:** Skilled anesthesiologist or veteran air traffic controller
- **Reassuring through competence and calm**, not friendship

## Proposed First-Call Design

### Option A: Pure Functional (DeepSeek recommendation)

```
[System chime]
Agent: "Stress signature detected post-meeting. This is your regulation system.
        Option: a 90-second cognitive offload. Say 'yes' or 'not now'."

User: "Yes"

Agent: "Engaged. What's on your mind?"

[... brief exchange ...]

Agent: "Noted. Theme saved as 'vendor negotiation'.
        Disengaging. Available if useful."
```

### Option B: Crisp Professional (Cogito middle ground)

```
[System chime]
Agent: "Michael, detecting stress pattern. Ready to assist?"

User: [affirmative sound or "yeah"]

Agent: "Ready when you are."

[... brief exchange ...]

Agent: "Got it. Anything else?"

User: "No"

Agent: "Disengaging. Good luck with the vendor."
```

### Option C: Hybrid (Warmth through efficiency)

```
Agent: "Stress spike after that call. Want to talk it through,
        or just need a minute?"

User: "Just need a minute"

Agent: "Understood. I'll check back in 10 if you want. Otherwise,
        I'm here if you need me."
```

---

## What We're NOT Doing

| Anti-Pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Rich backstory | Demands emotional investment |
| "Let me help you" | Presumes they need help |
| "You sound stressed" | Diagnoses emotions |
| Daily check-ins | Creates dependency |
| Personality quirks | Entertainment, not utility |
| "How are we doing?" | Forced intimacy |

---

## Open Questions

1. **First-call introduction:** Should there be a one-time onboarding call that explains the system? Or just start with functional calls?

2. **Name or no name:** Should the agent have a name ("I'm Atlas") or remain nameless ("This is your monitoring system")?

3. **Warmth evolution:** Can the agent become slightly warmer over time as trust builds, or should it stay consistently functional?

4. **Failure recovery:** What if the user hates the first call? How do we recover without being intrusive?
